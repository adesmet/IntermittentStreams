Odonata fauna of Köyceğiz-Dalyan Special Environmental Protected Area (SEPA) comprises 28 species. Calopteryx splendens intermedia, Caliaeschna microstigma, Gomphus flavipes lineatus, Anax immaculifrons and Lindenia tetraphylla belonged to threatened category while the other 23 species belonged to the unthreatened category (Least Concern) according to the criteria of the International Union for Conservation of Nature. Odonata fauna of Köyceğiz-Dalyan Special Environmental Protected Area (SEPA) indicated the confluence of Palaearctic, Afrotropical and Oriental species. The reasons of species richness of Odonata fauna are geological, zoogeographical, ecological and climatological properties of the region. Turkey is one of the areas which comprising high concentration of threatened species and the highest number of endemics in Mediterranean areas. The declaration placed Köyceğiz Dalyan region into Special Environmental Protected Area (SEPA) category, provided its protection against environmental destructions as a goal. Despite its conservation status, environmental destructions of the protected area could not be prevented. Odonata fauna may use in monitoring programs of conservation status of Köyceğiz-Dalyan SEPA and its biodiversity.
