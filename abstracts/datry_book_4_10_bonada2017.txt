Intermittent rivers and ephemeral streams (IRES) are ideal ecosystems in which to study how ecological and evolutionary processes interact across levels of organization, from genes to ecosystems. Recurrent disturbance and succession processes are strong drivers of evolution in IRES and occur at varying spatial and temporal scales. Species inhabiting IRES exhibit many adaptations that are subject to trade-offs related to flow intermittence. Flow intermittence affects genetic and species diversity, speciation, and extinction, all of which affect ecosystem functions.
