Purpose/Overview:

A PhD literature review is a critical assessment of the literature in your field and related to your specific research topic. The theoretical framework is the 'toolbox' you use to address the problem identified in your literature review, detailing the theories, propositions, hypotheses and concepts that you will use to address or make sense of this problem.

Document Type: PhD Cheat Sheet/Educational Guide
Created by: Dr. Maximilian Lemprière (thephdproofreaders.com)

Main Content Sections:
1. What is a Literature Review - definition, what to avoid, what to include, and guidance on making it great
2. What is a Theoretical Framework - definition, what it should include, what to avoid, and how it relates to the literature review
3. Practical guidance on writing both chapters with focus on critical thinking and proper academic framing
