A hydrological investigation of the relationship between the surface- and groundwater of the Kršid catchment and the spring water of the karst river, the Krka, is presented. The discharge along the Kršid decreases from the spring to the outlet (waterfall at the river Krka spring). Only in flood situations, some 30--60 days a year are normal hydrological relations found with discharge increasing towards the outlet. The Kršid river dries up regularly every year, with the number of days of zero flow increasing from the spring towards the outlet. A high degree of interdependence between groundwater levels in the Kršid catchment and the discharge of the Krka springs has been proved.
