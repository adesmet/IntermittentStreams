This sampling protocol is intended for use by members of the general public, agencies or NGOs wanting to collect water samples for environmental DNA (eDNA) analysis to detect aquatic species. The lab assays are able to detect extremely small concentrations of eDNA. Care must be taken to avoid contamination of samples with target species DNA accidentally transferred from other objects or waterbodies, as DNA contamination during field sampling can result in false detections. It is important to be attentive of contamination risks and follow the procedures of this protocol.
