Austria's Water in Numbers

The annual Hydrographical Yearbooks provide a detailed description of water quantities in Austria. They form an excellent and indispensable basis for water management. They also capture special hydrological phenomena, such as floods, glacier retreat, or periods of drought. This is based on a comprehensive Austria-wide measurement network that is continuously monitored. There is excellent cooperation between local observers and federal and state authorities. Currently, the data is also made available to interested parties on the homepage of the Ministry.

This yearbook of Austria depicts the year 2010. The collected data were carefully analyzed, verified, and compiled. This is already the 118th volume published in this series. The results show that the total precipitation in 2010 corresponded to the average across Austria. However, there were greater spatial and temporal differences in precipitation distribution. A multitude of regional heavy rainfall events led to localized flooding and debris flows with sometimes considerable damage.
