Most fish species in intermittent rivers and ephemeral streams (IRES) survive by using a combination of adaptable colonization and recruitment strategies. A few species possess specialized adaptations such as air-breathing that allow them to persist in extreme IRES. Fish in IRES are threatened by increased habitat fragmentation (due to factors such as river regulation, water abstraction, and climate change) and the presence of alien species. Preservation of IRES with natural flow patterns should be a global management priority to conserve specialized biota such as fish.
