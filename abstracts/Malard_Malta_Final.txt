Characterisation of groundwater habitats: the faunal perspective

This presentation by Florian Malard from CNRS/University of Lyon, France, addresses the characterisation of groundwater ecosystems from a faunal perspective. The work focuses on bringing groundwater ecosystems into the Water Framework Directive (WFD) and examines whether ten years of research has provided better understanding of groundwater ecosystem quality criteria. Key topics include habitat typology (EU and national levels), habitat-specific biological references, scoring habitat quality, and the development of species occurrence datasets. The presentation emphasizes the need for habitat typology definitions, criteria development, and scoring frameworks to assess groundwater ecosystem status relative to reference conditions, particularly for invertebrate communities.
